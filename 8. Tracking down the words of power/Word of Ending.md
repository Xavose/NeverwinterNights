# ⚫ Word of Ending

## “The Silence After”

### Core Theme

Entropy. Finality. The absolute end.

This should feel horrifyingly  *quiet* .

---

# 📖 Premise

Entire places are disappearing.

Not destroyed.

Erased.

People forget cities existed.

Magic fails in affected areas.

Even gods avoid speaking of:

# The Hollow Places

---

# 🧠 The Truth

The Word of Ending was the final failsafe of the Creator Race.

If creation became corrupted:

it could be unmade.

Morag seeks it because:

> a god cannot oppose what no longer exists.

---

# 🕳️ Quest Structure

## Phase 1 — The Hollow

The party investigates a vanished village.

What they find:

* empty foundations,
* colorless landscapes,
* no sound,
* no souls.

Even memory becomes unstable.

### Mechanic

While inside Hollow zones:

* healing is halved,
* summoned creatures vanish,
* spoken words fade unnaturally quickly.

---

# 📚 Discovery

Ancient texts reveal:

## “Where the Word is spoken, existence loosens.”

The Word resides in:

# The Last Library

A structure hidden beyond reality’s edge.

---

# 🚪 Phase 2 — Journey Beyond Reality

To reach it:

the party must travel through places where reality weakens.

Examples:

* staircases leading nowhere,
* oceans suspended upside down,
* stars frozen motionless.

---

# 🏛️ The Last Library

Visual Style:

* endless black shelves,
* books with blank pages,
* floating fragments of erased worlds,
* silent librarians made of ash.

---

# 🧩 Central Mechanic — Unraveling

The closer they get:

the less real they become.

Examples:

* shadows disappear,
* names fade from memory,
* equipment loses details,
* players briefly vanish from scenes.

---

# 👤 Main Guardian

## The Final Witness

The last surviving archivist of erased realities.

It believes:

> existence inevitably leads to suffering.

---

# ⚔️ Final Encounter

The battle occurs in a collapsing reality-space.

Each round:

parts of the battlefield cease existing.

Mechanics:

* terrain disappears,
* spells fail randomly,
* erased creatures leave no corpses.

---

# 🕯️ The Word Itself

The Word of Ending is not heard.

It is:

## the absence of sound.

To claim it:

someone must willingly speak a true ending:

* abandoning revenge,
* accepting mortality,
* releasing grief.

---

# ⚫ Reward

The Word grants:

* anti-magic effects,
* banishment,
* severing magical bonds,
* the ability to permanently destroy extraplanar entities.
