# Word of Death

*Theme: mortality, the gift of endings, and the eye that sees what all living things must face.*

Artifacts of the Dawn War scattered across Faerûn. Each word follows three steps—**locate**, **attune**, **claim**—which the party may pursue in flexible order, though location generally precedes meaningful attunement. Effects at each tier are **additive**.

---

## Base Form

**Requires:** No attunement

**Form:** An ancient skull

**Effects:**

- While the word is in your possession, any damage you deal as part of an attack or spell is necrotic damage. This applies to additional effects that might add damage to that spell or attack. Such as a class feature or a magic weapon that deals bonus damage.

---

## Initial Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Understand the cost of death

**Form:** A large black crow

**Effects:**

- While attuned to the word, once per turn you can use your bonus action to turn the eye of death upon another creature. Until the start of your next turn that creature has vulnerability to necrotic damage. If they are immune or resistant to necrotic damage they lose that immunity or resistance for the same period of time.

---

## Advanced Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Understand the gift of death

**Form:** An ancient scythe

**Effects:**

- While attuned to the word, you can cast *power word kill*. Once you have used this feature, at the end of every long rest you may roll 1d6. On a 6, you regain the use of this feature.
- While attuned to the word, you can use CON, DEX, STR, CHA, INT, or WIS as the stat used to calculate attack rolls and damage rolls with this weapon. When you score a critical hit with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 20 CHA saving throw or immediately die.

---

## Full Attunement

**Requires:** Does not require attunement (internalized as a feat)

**How to unlock:** Die while attuned to the word

**Form:** The word becomes a feat the character instantly gains access to.

**Feat — Word of Death:**

- You can cause the word to appear in your outstretched hand as a weapon of death (no action required).
- Your soul has become synonymous with death. If you die, roll 1d6; you are instead brought back to life as if by the effects of the *reincarnate* spell. The value you rolled on the d6 determines how long it takes for you to be brought back to life in this way:
  - **6** — 1 second
  - **5** — 1 minute
  - **4** — 1 hour
  - **3** — 1 week
  - **2** — 1 month
  - **1** — 1 year

In this time, the word of power reverts to its base form and can be claimed by any other during that period.
