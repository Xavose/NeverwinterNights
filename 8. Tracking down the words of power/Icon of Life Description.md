# Icon of Life

*Theme: the war was won because mortals lived—growth, renewal, and the refusal to let existence end.*

Artifacts of the Dawn War scattered across Faerûn. Each word follows three steps—**locate**, **attune**, **claim**—which the party may pursue in flexible order, though location generally precedes meaningful attunement. Effects at each tier are **additive**.

---

## Base Form

**Requires:** No attunement

**Form:** A jade seed in a clay reliquary, or a wooden heart studded with amber

**Effects:**

- While you possess it, you have advantage on death saving throws.
- Once per long rest, you can touch a creature and end one disease or the poisoned condition on it, or stabilize a dying creature without a medicine check.

---

## Initial Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Restore life or its promise without using a spell slot—e.g. reunite a family, regrow a blighted grove, or return a soul's memory to the world even if the body cannot be saved

**Form:** A living staff (wood still grows tiny leaves) or a silver chalice that fills with clear water each dawn

**Effects:**

- While attuned, as a bonus action once per turn, touch a creature within 5 feet: it regains 1d8 + proficiency bonus hit points, or you end blindness or deafness on it. Uses equal to proficiency bonus per long rest (total healing per rest capped at 5 × your level).

---

## Advanced Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Choose life when death is easier—spare a mass murderer who begs, bear a plague victim's pain, or stay behind so others live

**Form:** A mace or sickle of living wood and crystal (1d8 bludgeoning or slashing, +1; counts as a spellcasting focus for druid, ranger, or cleric spells if you use one)

**Effects:**

- You can cast *revivify* once per long rest without material components (the icon provides the diamond's worth).
- When you hit an undead with the icon weapon, it takes an extra 2d8 radiant damage.
- Once per long rest, you can cast *aura of life* without concentration for 1 minute (once only; when it ends, you can't cast leveled spells except cantrips until the end of your next turn).

---

## Full Attunement

**Requires:** Does not require attunement (internalized as a feat)

**How to unlock:** Survive true death's threshold—0 HP and three failed death saves, or *power word kill*—and return through the icon's power once (narrative: a god of life notices)

**Form:** The word becomes a feat the character instantly gains access to.

**Feat — Heart of the Icon:**

- Manifest the icon as weapon or chalice (bonus action).
- You are immune to disease; you age at half rate (flavor; rarely matters mechanically).
- **Capstone with risk:** When a creature you can see within 30 feet dies, you can use your reaction to attempt one resurrection: if it died within the last minute and isn't undead, it returns with 1 hit point and 3 levels of exhaustion. You gain 2 levels of exhaustion and the icon reverts to base form for 1d6 days (roll at end of each long rest; on a 6, it returns to you in base form only). During those days, you cannot benefit from any Icon of Life tier and another may claim the base icon.
