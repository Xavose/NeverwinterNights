# Word of Bewilderment

*Theme: the Dawn War was also fought in minds—false paths, shattered certainties, enemies who could not trust their own senses.*

Artifacts of the Dawn War scattered across Faerûn. Each word follows three steps—**locate**, **attune**, **claim**—which the party may pursue in flexible order, though location generally precedes meaningful attunement. Effects at each tier are **additive**.

---

## Base Form

**Requires:** No attunement

**Form:** A mirror shard in a velvet wrap, or a clay tablet whose script shifts when unread

**Effects:**

- While you possess it, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed.
- Once per short or long rest, when a creature you can see makes an attack roll against you, you can force it to roll 1d4 and subtract the result (no reaction).

---

## Initial Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Unravel a lie that held a community together—or expose a truth so destabilizing that someone important breaks down (compassion still required; this isn't cruelty for its own sake)

**Form:** A mask of smoked glass, or a whispering coin that flips itself

**Effects:**

- While attuned, as a bonus action once per turn, choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. Until the start of your next turn, the next attack roll against it has disadvantage, or the next attack roll it makes has disadvantage (your choice when you use it).

---

## Advanced Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Navigate a labyrinth of illusion, dream, or conspiracy without losing your sense of self (e.g. *maze*, *mind blank* pressure, or an entire arc inside a false memory)

**Form:** A rapier of smoky glass (1d8 piercing, +1, finesse) or a rod that hums with wrong frequencies

**Effects:**

- You can cast *confusion* once per long rest (WIS save DC 8 + proficiency bonus + INT, WIS, or CHA—choose when you first reach this tier).
- When you hit with the weapon, you can deal an extra 2d6 psychic damage and the target can't take reactions until the start of its next turn (uses equal to proficiency bonus per long rest).
- You have advantage on initiative rolls and on checks to detect illusions or hidden creatures.

---

## Full Attunement

**Requires:** Does not require attunement (internalized as a feat)

**How to unlock:** Lose something you trusted—an identity, ally, memory, or certainty—and rebuild anyway (real character cost, not amnesia-as-gag)

**Form:** The word becomes a feat the character instantly gains access to.

**Feat — Unsay the Real:**

- Manifest the word as weapon or rod (bonus action).
- Once per long rest, you can cast *synaptic static* or *hallucinatory terrain* (your choice when you gain the feat).
- **Capstone with risk:** Once per long rest, you can speak the Word at a creature you can see within 60 feet: it must succeed on a WIS save (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen mental stat) or suffer **bewilderment** for 1 minute. While bewildered, at the start of each of its turns it must roll 1d6: on 1–3 it wastes its action; on 4–6 it acts normally but has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns; three successful saves end the effect. You have disadvantage on Wisdom saves until your next long rest, and the word reverts to base form for 1d4 days, during which you are vulnerable to psychic damage and another may claim it.
