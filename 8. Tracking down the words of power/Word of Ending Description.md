# Word of Ending

*Theme: closure—not murder, but the last note of a song, the seal on a gate, the final line of a spell or era. Pairs with death but is the period at the end of the sentence.*

Artifacts of the Dawn War scattered across Faerûn. Each word follows three steps—**locate**, **attune**, **claim**—which the party may pursue in flexible order, though location generally precedes meaningful attunement. Effects at each tier are **additive**.

---

## Base Form

**Requires:** No attunement

**Form:** A black quill that never runs dry, or a bronze seal ring cracked down the middle

**Effects:**

- While you possess it, when you cast a spell or hit with an attack, you can declare **ending** on one magical effect on the target (e.g. *shield*, *haste*, bless from an item). The target must succeed on a save (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + your spellcasting modifier, or CHA if none) or that effect ends. Once per short or long rest.

---

## Initial Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Bring a proper ending to something that should have finished long ago—a ghost, a curse loop, an endless duel, a debt, a prophecy's stale chapter

**Form:** A broken hourglass suspended in crystal, or a single page of a book that burns away as it is read

**Effects:**

- While attuned, as a bonus action once per turn, mark one creature you can see within 60 feet with **Ending Mark** until the start of your next turn. The first time it takes damage, it takes an extra 1d6 force damage, and if it is concentrating on a spell, it has disadvantage on the concentration save from that damage.

---

## Advanced Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** End something large at a cost—close a planar rift, finish a ritual that unmakes an artifact, or sever a god-touched binding (the party should feel the weight)

**Form:** A greataxe or war pick of dull iron (1d12 slashing, +1) inscribed with a final character in Celestial

**Effects:**

- Once per long rest you can cast *dispel magic* at 5th level as an action (no components).
- Once per long rest, when you hit a creature with this weapon, you can attempt to end one condition on it (charmed, frightened, paralyzed, etc.) or end one condition it imposed on another—if you are ending a condition *on the enemy*, they take 4d8 force damage instead.
- Critical hits against aberrations, fey, or undead deal an extra 2d8 force damage.

---

## Full Attunement

**Requires:** Does not require attunement (internalized as a feat)

**How to unlock:** Accept an ending of your own story's chapter—retire a title, break a pact, destroy your own masterpiece, or let an NPC you love leave the tale

**Form:** The word becomes a feat the character instantly gains access to.

**Feat — Final Word:**

- Manifest the Word as weapon or seal (bonus action).
- You can sense active spells and magical effects within 30 feet (not exact identification, but awareness that magic is present and its broad school).
- **Capstone with risk:** Once per long rest, you speak the Word of Ending. Choose one:
  - **End Magic:** A creature you can see within 60 feet must succeed on a CHA save (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + CHA) or all spells of 5th level or lower on it end and it can't cast leveled spells until the end of its next turn.
  - **End Battle:** All creatures of your choice in a 20-foot radius centered on a point within 60 feet must succeed on a WIS save (same DC) or be unable to take the Attack action until the end of your next turn.

After either option, you gain one level of exhaustion, lose access to all Word of Ending benefits for 1d6 days (the word becomes base form, claimable), and you have disadvantage on death saves until you complete an ending-ritual (defeat a boss, finish a quest line, or narratively close the book on that downtime).
