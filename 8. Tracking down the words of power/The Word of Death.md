# ☠️ Word of Death

## “The Last Shepherd”

### Core Theme

Death is not evil.

Death is  *necessary* .

The Word has been hidden by a missing reaper because something in the cosmos has gone terribly wrong.

---

# 📖 Premise

For months across the Sword Coast:

* corpses do not decay,
* spirits linger,
* resurrection magic misfires,
* some people simply  *cannot die* .

The party eventually learns:

> Thracier disappeared while carrying the Word of Death.

Without the Word being spoken, the boundary between life and death is collapsing.

---

# 🎭 The Twist

The reaper did not “steal” the Word.

He hid it because:

> Morag cannot ascend if true death still exists.

If Morag gains the Word of Death, she could:

* permanently slay gods,
* erase souls from the cycle,
* sever the Fugue Plane entirely.

The reaper chose cosmic imbalance over catastrophe.

---

# 🕯️ Quest Structure

## Phase 1 — The Dead Who Remain

### Investigation Arc

The party investigates:

* villages haunted by confused spirits,
* a battlefield where soldiers endlessly relive death,
* an immortal murderer begging for execution.

### Skill Challenge Ideas

* Religion → interpret funerary rites
* Medicine → determine why bodies do not decay
* Insight → speak with trapped souls
* Arcana → trace necrotic distortions

Successes reveal:

* souls are not reaching **Fugue Plane**
* something is intercepting death itself

---

## Phase 2 — Finding the Shepherd

The party learns of an ancient reaper:

# Thracier, The Last Shepherd

He once guided souls before the gods of death existed.

He vanished into:

## The Vale Between Heartbeats

A hidden demiplane accessible only:

* at the exact moment a creature dies willingly,
* during an eclipse,
* while the Word of Travel or similar magic is invoked.

This demiplane is being used by Morag to siphon the souls of the dead to fuel the source stone.

---

# 🌫️ The Vale Between Heartbeats

### Visual Tone

* Ash fields
* Still air
* Countless silent spirits
* Black stars overhead
* Giant stone doorways leading nowhere

Time barely moves here.

---

# ⚔️ Encounters

## The Unburied

Souls fused to their corpses because death has stalled.

### Mechanics

* Enemies revive unless “laid to rest”
* Players must perform rites mid-combat

---

## The River Without End

A puzzle encounter.

The party must cross a river made from memories of the dead.

Each player sacrifices:

* a proficient skill,
* a proficient saving throw,
* or Max HP (Charisma Mod Per Level).

## The Cult of Origin Attacks

If the cult is able to learn of the party's intentions to seek out the Word of Death the attack while the party is on the Ferry.

Maugrim appears on the deck and tries to deal with the ferryman to not allow the party to continue forward. He tells the ferryman that his master, Morag, will agree to sever the chains that bind his boat to the River Without End. If the party is not able to convince the Ferryman to help them, he sends them ashore and takes Maugrim to the word of power. If the party does succeed, Maugrim summons forth a wave of undead at the party as he escapes. 

Use: Jarad Vod Savo stat block
