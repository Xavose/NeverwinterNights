# Glyph of Three Blades

*Theme: war as triad—strike, guard, finish; three mortal champions acting as one in the Dawn War.*

Artifacts of the Dawn War scattered across Faerûn. Each word follows three steps—**locate**, **attune**, **claim**—which the party may pursue in flexible order, though location generally precedes meaningful attunement. Effects at each tier are **additive**.

---

## Base Form

**Requires:** No attunement

**Form:** A stone plaque or iron bracer etched with three overlapping blades

**Effects:**

- While you possess it, when you take the Attack action, you can treat one weapon attack as if you had advantage on the roll. Once you use this, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

---

## Initial Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Honor three distinct oaths in one arc—e.g. debt to an ally, mercy to a foe who surrendered, and truth to a lie that harmed innocents (define the three at your table; each must cost something)

**Form:** Three throwing knives fused at the hilts, or a triple-bladed dagger (light, finesse, thrown 20/60, 1d4 piercing for one attack roll)

**Effects:**

- While attuned, once per turn when you take the Attack action, you can make one additional weapon attack with the glyph weapon as part of that action (no bonus action). The extra attack does not stack with Extra Attack beyond this benefit.

---

## Advanced Attunement

**Requires:** Attunement

**How to unlock:** Defeat a worthy foe in three exchanges—three rounds of direct duel, three named champions, or three phases of a boss (narrative framing)

**Form:** A pair of shortswords and a main-guard dagger that orbit your belt as one set, or a glaive with two side blades (martial, reach 10 ft., 1d10 slashing, +1)

**Effects:**

- Once per long rest, for 1 minute, when you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can immediately make one weapon attack against a different creature within reach (or within thrown range) as part of the same attack action chain.
- When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with the glyph weapon, you can move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Threefold parry:** When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to add 1d6 + proficiency bonus to your AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, the attacker takes 1d6 slashing damage. Uses equal to proficiency bonus per long rest.

---

## Full Attunement

**Requires:** Does not require attunement (internalized as a feat)

**How to unlock:** Accept that victory may require your ending—deliver the third blade (finishing blow) on a foe who could destroy a city, knowing you may be maimed, captured, or lose the weapon afterward

**Form:** The word becomes a feat the character instantly gains access to.

**Feat — Triune Strike:**

- Manifest the glyph as any of its advanced weapons (bonus action).
- Once per turn when you hit with a weapon attack, you can apply one of: push 10 ft., knock prone (STR save DC 8 + proficiency bonus + STR or DEX), or impose disadvantage on the target's next attack before the end of your next turn.
- **Capstone with risk:** Once per long rest, when you hit with a weapon attack, you can declare **Third Blade**: the attack is a critical hit, and the target must succeed on a CON save (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + your highest physical modifier) or suffer a lasting wound—speed halved, disadvantage on concentration checks, no regeneration—until *greater restoration* or 30 days. You gain one level of exhaustion immediately, and the word reverts to base form until you re-earn it by besting a skilled warrior in single combat (no magic items; allies may watch but not help).
