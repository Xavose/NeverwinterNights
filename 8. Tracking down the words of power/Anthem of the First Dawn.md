# 🌅 Anthem of the First Dawn

## “When the Sun First Sang”

### Core Theme

Hope, creation, primordial light.

This should feel  *mythic and beautiful* .

---

# 📖 Premise

Before the sun existed, the world was illuminated by:

## The First Dawn Song

A sung Word of Creation.

The Anthem is hidden within:

# The Choir Radiant

Ancient celestial beings who witnessed the first sunrise.

---

# ☀️ Quest Structure

## Phase 1 — Following Ancient Light

The party tracks:

* dawn phenomena,
* ancient solar temples,
* creatures spontaneously singing unknown hymns.

Research reveals:

> The Anthem is not spoken.

It is  *remembered collectively* .

---

# 🏔️ Phase 2 — The Climb

The Choir Radiant dwells atop:

## Mount Aurelion

A mountain that only appears at sunrise.

### Trials During Ascent

Each represents an aspect of dawn:

* Hope
* Renewal
* Sacrifice
* Mercy

---

# 🧩 Trial Ideas

## Trial of Renewal

Players must heal enemies instead of killing them.

---

## Trial of Sacrifice

To proceed:

someone must willingly surrender:

* a magic item,
* cherished memory,
* years of lifespan.

---

# 👼 The Choir Radiant

Not angels exactly.

More like living embodiments of sunrise.

Describe:

* bodies made of gold-white light,
* voices harmonizing unconsciously,
* eyes containing morning skies.

---

# 🎵 Final Trial

The Anthem cannot simply be learned.

The party must:

## Add their own voices to creation.

Ask each player:

> “What future is worth fighting for?”

Their answers literally shape the ritual.

---

# ⚔️ Optional Finale

Morag’s servants attack during the ritual.

The party must defend the Choir while completing the Anthem.

---

# 🌄 Reward

The Anthem grants:

* protection from despair/fear,
* radiant empowerment,
* the ability to “awaken” dormant goodness,
* temporary suppression of corruption.
