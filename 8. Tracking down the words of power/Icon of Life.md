# 🌱 Icon of Life

## “The Garden Beneath the World”

### Core Theme

Life is not kindness.

Life is relentless.

This quest should force the party to confront the terrifying *persistence* of life itself.

---

# 📖 Premise

Across the North:

* forests grow overnight,
* corpses sprout flowers,
* diseases evolve unnaturally fast,
* monsters regenerate endlessly.

Druids claim:

> “Something beneath the world has awakened.”

The party eventually discovers:

## the Icon of Life is not a word that is spoken.

It is a *symbol* etched into creation itself.

---

# 🧠 The Truth

Before the gods shaped mortal life, the Creator Race cultivated:

# The First Garden

A living engine designed to endlessly generate new forms of life.

The Icon was its control sigil.

Now Morag seeks it because:

* Life energy can stabilize divine ascension,
* It can regenerate her mortal form during apotheosis,
* It can allow her to survive the strain of the other Words.

---

# 🌿 Quest Structure

## Phase 1 — The Blooming Dead

The party investigates bizarre phenomena:

* A battlefield consumed by flowers growing from corpses
* Wolves splitting into multiple smaller wolves when slain
* Villagers mutating into plant-like beings

### Skill Challenge

* Nature → identify impossible growth patterns
* Medicine → determine regenerative anomalies
* Arcana → trace primal magical resonance
* Survival → track overgrown wildlife

Success reveals:

> all life is being drawn downward.

---

# ⛰️ Phase 2 — Descent Into the Rootways

The party travels beneath the earth into:

# The Rootways

Ancient tunnels woven from roots miles thick.

Visuals:

* glowing sap rivers,
* heartbeat-like tremors,
* forests growing upside down,
* giant embryonic creatures inside bark cocoons.

---

# 🧩 Environmental Mechanic — Overflowing Life

Life energy becomes excessive.

Examples:

* Healing magic overheals and causes mutations
* Blood spilled spawns vines
* Long rests cause strange dreams and temporary transformations

---

# 🐉 Midpoint Encounter

## The Thousandborn Stag

A massive guardian constantly birthing smaller versions of itself.

Mechanic:

Every round:

* pieces sever off,
* grow into independent creatures,
* and continue fighting.

The party must discover:

> the Icon sustains all nearby life symbiotically.

---

# 🌳 Final Area — The First Garden

A colossal underground biome.

At its center:

## The Verdant Heart

A tree larger than a city.

Its roots pulse with green-gold runes:

the Icon of Life.

---

# 👁️ Final Dilemma

The Garden speaks through all nearby living things.

It asks:

> “Why should mortality exist?”

The party learns:

* death was introduced *later* into creation,
* the original world may have been immortal.

---

# ⚔️ Final Encounter Options

## Option 1 — Fight the Garden Avatar

A colossal humanoid made of roots, flowers, bone, and flesh.

---

## Option 2 — Bind With the Icon

A player temporarily merges with primal life.

Possible consequences:

* rapid healing,
* inability to willingly kill,
* plant-like mutations.

---

# 🌱 Reward

The Icon grants:

* massive restorative power,
* temporary resurrection,
* immunity to disease/poison,
* the ability to awaken dormant life.
