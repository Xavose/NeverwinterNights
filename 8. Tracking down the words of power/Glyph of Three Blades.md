# ⚔️ Glyph of Three Blades

## “The War That Split Heaven”

### Core Theme

Conflict as a shaping force.

Not war for destruction—

war as the engine of history.

---

# 📖 Premise

Ancient battlefields across Faerûn awaken:

* spectral armies march again,
* old grudges reignite,
* warriors dream of a three-pointed sigil burning in the sky.

The party learns of:

# The Triune Glyph

A Creator Race rune used to command war itself.

---

# 🧠 The Truth

The Glyph was divided long ago into:

* Blade of Victory
* Blade of Sacrifice
* Blade of Dominion

Separated because together they could:

* command armies absolutely,
* reshape nations,
* wound even gods.

Morag seeks it to conquer resistance during ascension.

---

# 🗡️ Quest Structure

The quest is divided into:

## Three War Trials

Each blade resides in a different battlefield-memory realm.

---

# ⚔️ Trial One — Victory

## The Crimson Field

A frozen moment from an ancient war.

The party fights through impossible odds.

### Twist

The enemy commander offers surrender.

To gain the blade:

the party must choose:

* mercy,
* or ruthless victory.

The Glyph remembers their choice.

---

# 🩸 Trial Two — Sacrifice

## The Bridge of Thousands

The party relives the final stand of forgotten heroes.

Mechanic:

Victory is impossible unless:

someone stays behind.

Not necessarily permanently—but the trial demands willingness.

---

# 👑 Trial Three — Dominion

## The Iron Court

A realm where conflict is resolved through command.

The party must lead spectral armies.

### Mechanics

* tactical encounters,
* morale systems,
* battlefield objectives.

This lets martial characters shine in unusual ways.

---

# 🧩 The Combined Glyph

After earning all three:

the sigils merge into:

# The Glyph of Three Blades

A burning triangular rune hovering in the air.

---

# 👁️ Final Revelation

The Glyph speaks:

> “War does not create evil. It reveals it.”

The party realizes:

every wielder shapes the Glyph differently.

---

# ⚔️ Final Encounter

Morag’s forces attempt to seize the Glyph during its reforging.

The battlefield shifts dynamically between:

* all three trial realms simultaneously.

---

# ⚔️ Reward

The Glyph grants:

* battlefield command powers,
* spectral weapon manifestations,
* tactical foresight,
* the ability to challenge powerful foes to binding duels.

---

# 💡 Big Picture Advice

You now have Words representing:

* Life
* Death
* Beginning
* Ending
* War
* Identity

That’s excellent because your campaign is evolving into:

> a struggle over the *fundamental laws of reality.*

At this point, your players are basically engaging in proto-divine mythmaking—which is exactly where an epic **Neverwinter Nights** continuation should end up.
