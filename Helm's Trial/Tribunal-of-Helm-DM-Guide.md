# Helm's Trial — The Celestial Stairway (DM Guide)

A return adventure for **Gariel** (Divine Soul sorcerer, Helm) and **guest PCs** who exist only for this arc. **Torm** summons Gariel to **Trueheart** and tasks him with investigating corruption on the **Celestial Stairway** (Infinite Staircase). **Tyriel** (Tyr in disguise; same being as **Tyrin** at Ashbone Manor) tags along to watch the vessel who holds Helm's hidden power.

**Related notes:** `0 - Game Specific Notes/Banske Game/Characters/Gariel.md`, `A4 Bastion/Ashbone Manor.md`, `8. Tracking down the words of power/`, `99 The BBEGs/`.

---

## 1. Premise & Lore

### 1.1 Helm and the Stairway

**Helm** was the **Protector of the Celestial Stairway**—the **Infinite Staircase**, a network of paths linking realms and planes. During the **Time of Troubles**, when Ao sent the gods to walk among mortals, **Helm alone remained** at his post so no deity could use the Stairway to **ascend** again.

With Helm **gone**, the Stairway has **no clear successor**. **Morag** orchestrated Helm's fall (via Cyric's deception of Tyr) so the Stairway would stay **unguarded** long enough for her to use it in her **ascension** plot.

### 1.2 Torm's burden

**Torm** (duty, loyalty, righteousness, law, valor) has taken **part-time** stewardship of the Stairway, but his portfolio is too vast to defend every landing. He needs a **mortal agent** with a living bond to Helm: **Gariel**.

### 1.3 Cyric's plot (this adventure's villain)

**Cyric** tricked **Tyr** into killing Helm. His goals now:

1. Keep the Stairway **twisted** so travelers no longer reach **correct** destinations.
2. **Claim Everwatch** (Helm's divine realm) as a new seat of power—**far-seeing** surveillance beyond his normal reach.
3. Eventually seize Helm's **remaining** power (much of it is **locked inside Gariel**; Tyr holds another portion per your canon).

**Morag** benefits from all of this but may only appear as **rumor, omen, or a refugee's half-true tale** in this one-shot unless you want an explicit name-drop at Everwatch.

### 1.4 Design goals

| Goal             | How this adventure serves it                                                                                                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gariel spotlight | Summons, Helm resonance on the Stairway, final confrontation choices.                                                                 |
| Guest engagement | Torm-assigned companions with**Stairway boons**; entrance branches reward different skills.                                     |
| Tyr drama        | Tyriel as protector and secret keeper;**unmasking at Everwatch** when Cyric sees through him.                                   |
| Campaign bridge  | Corruption source named; optional**Word of Power** clue; Gariel may **unlock** Helm power if Tyr imbues him in the fight. |

**Suggested length:** 1 session (4–5 hr, rush one entrance) or **2–3 sessions** (all three entrances as rumors + one chosen, full Inn, Everwatch).

**Level:** Match Gariel. Guests at same level or **one lower**.

## 2. Session Zero (5 minutes)

- Guest PCs are **not** main-campaign characters; they are **sworn to Torm for this mission** and return to Trueheart (or fade from the tale) when the arc ends.
- **Tyriel** is an **NPC** you play—do not PvP him unless the table invites it.
- **Gariel** knows Helm is **dead**; dreams (grey / blue / gold in `Gariel.md`) may **echo** on the Stairway—use as foreshadowing, not required exposition.
- Confirm **which Sword Coast entrance** the table will use (or vote in Act II). You do not need to prep all three in full detail for session one—prep **one** deeply, skim the others.

### 4.1 Frame

Torm assigns **2–4 companions** from his service: paladins, war clerics, planar scouts, or **Trueheart aspirants** who owe a **debt of duty**. They are **equals** on the mission, not Gariel's bodyguards—but the **quest** is his summons.

| Boon                           | Effect                                                                                                                                              |

## 5. Core Mechanics

### 5.1 Stairway distortion (party tracker)

Track **Distortion** from **0** to **5**. At **5**, the next transition sends the party to a **hostile** or **comic wrong** landing (DM picks); then reset to **3**.

| Event                                                           | Distortion |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Enter the Stairway from Material Plane                          | +1         |
| Failed navigation check (§5.3)                                 | +1         |
| Accept Cyric-tainted bargain (Bane oath, false shortcut at Inn) | +2         |
| Use Halaster's “shortcut” without verification                | +1         |
| Break Elminster's wards**carelessly** (see §6.3b)        | +1         |

**Reduce Distortion:** Successful **Investigation** or **Arcana** at Nowhere Inn (§7); **Ratatoskr** ride (−1 once); **Gariel's Helm resonance** (−1 once per session).

### 5.2 Helm's resonance (Gariel only)

When Gariel is on the Stairway or in **Everwatch**, he may use **Helm's resonance** (no action, **once per short rest**):

- **Sense:** Learn whether a door/path leads **“as intended”** or **“bent.”** (DM answers honestly.)
- **Push:** **DC 14 Cha** (proficiency if proficient in Religion): **Dispel** a minor illusion on one object, or give **advantage** to one ally's next save vs. charm/illusion.

**Unlock (finale):** If Tyr **imbues** Gariel during Cyric fight, grant one of:

- **+1** spell attack / save DC for divine spells until long rest, or
- One extra use of **Helm's resonance** per short rest, or
- Known spell: *Warding bond* or *Beacon of hope* (once/long rest without slot)—pick what fits his build.

### 5.3 Navigation on the Stairway

When travel is **not** on Ratatoskr's back, each leg: **one** party member leads (usually Gariel).

- **Check:** **Wisdom (Survival)** or **Intelligence (Arcana)** **DC 14** (Distortion 3+: **DC 16**).
- **Success:** Arrive on time; **no** Distortion gain.
- **Failure:** +1 Distortion; **1d4** hours lost or wrong **minor** room (combat or roleplay).

**Ratatoskr's ride:** Auto-success to **Nowhere Inn**; **−1** Distortion once.

### 5.4 Ban's dark promise (Kilgrave only)

If Gariel swears to **kill one mortal when Bane chooses**:

- Record **Bane's Mark** (invisible to others until triggered).
- **Benefit:** Safe passage; **−1** Distortion when entering Stairway.
- **Cost:** Campaign clock—Bane will call the debt **before act III of main plot** (you decide when). **Breaking oath:** Curse—**disadvantage** on saves vs. fear until atoned (**DC 18** Religion at major temple).

## 6. Adventure Structure

### Act I — Summons in Trueheart (30–45 min)

**Scene:** Gariel (alone or at Ashbone) sees **golden gauntlet** sigils in fire/shadow; he is **pulled** to **Trueheart**—a realm of **white stone, banners, and measured thunder**.

**Torm's briefing (hit these beats):**

1. Helm guarded the **Celestial Stairway**; he is **gone**; **no successor**.
2. **Morag's** long game benefits from an **open** Stairway (optional detail level).
3. **Cyric's** corruption: destinations **lie**; refugees pile up at **Nowhere Inn**.
4. **Task:** Enter from the **Sword Coast**, find the **source**, report back—or **fix** it if you can.
5. **Companions:** Torm introduces guest PCs (present in Trueheart or **waiting** at the Material entrance).
6. **Tyriel:** “I was already riding for Neverwinter when the call went out.” Torm **accepts** him if he swears **limited oath** (duty, no abandonment of Gariel).

**Mechanics:**

- **DC 12 Wis save** or **awed** (no actions) for 1 round on arrival—guests too.
- Torm grants **Badge of Trueheart** (each PC): advantage on **first** Persuasion with **lawful good** planar beings this arc.

**Maps:** Open hall or **bridge over light**—abstract is fine.

**Prep:** Torm voice, guest PC introductions, Tyriel intro.

---

### Act II — Choose an Entrance (15 min + travel)

Present **three** known landings on the **Sword Coast** (handout or narration):

| Entrance                         | Location        | Hook                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A. Castle Kilgrave**     | Cormyr          | Bane's blackguards guard a**tainted** threshold  |
| **B. Temple of Lathander** | Shadowdale      | **Elminster's wards**; trapped **modrons** |
| **C. Griffon Eyrie**       | Mount Waterdeep | **Halaster** studies the breach                  |

**Travel time (narrative):** Kilgrave **3–5 days** from Neverwinter; Shadowdale **4–6**; Griffon Eyrie **1–2** (visible from Waterdeep).

**Run only the chosen branch** unless you have multiple sessions and want a **montage** of the other two as rumors.

---

### Act III — Entrance Encounters (60–90 min each)

#### 3A — Castle Kilgrave (Bane's blackguards)

**Setup:** Stairway mouth in a **cellar crypt** or **gatehouse shrine**. **3–5 blackguards** (see §3.5). Commander: **Sergeant Morvek** (name flexible).

**Roleplay:**

- They feel **familiar** energy on the Stairway—**like Bane's** but **wrong** (Cyric's **false tyranny** flavor). **Religion DC 14:** not Bane's true touch.
- **Offer:** Safe passage if **Gariel** swears the **death oath** (§5.4). They **honor** oaths—they are lawful evil, not chaotic.
- **Fight warning:** “Spill our blood and **Bane's eye** turns to you.”

**Combat (if fought):**

- **Blackguards** × number of PCs (max 6).
- **Morvek** has **Command** 1/day.
- **Difficulty:** Medium for level 5–7; Hard if undermanned.

**After passage:** Stone stairs **fade into impossible geometry**; **+1 Distortion** (§5.1).

**Prep:** Small dungeon map; blackguard stats; oath card handout.

---

#### 3B — Temple of Lathander, Shadowdale (Elminster's wards)

**Setup:** Basement **arch** once led to **Shadowfell**; monsters poured through until **Elminster** (at priesthood's plea) laid **layered wards**. Now the arch **bends** toward **Mechanus**; **modrons** are **stuck** half-in the seal.

**Roleplay:**

- **High Priestess Velara** asks for **help**, not vandalism.
- **Goal:** Lower wards **safely** to free modrons—not destroy the arch.

**Mechanics — ward puzzle (3 successes before 2 failures):**

| Approach                             | Skill         | DC |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------- | -- |
| Read Elminster's script              | Arcana        | 15 |
| Harmonize with Lathander's dawn rite | Religion      | 14 |
| Deduce lawful “key” sequence       | Investigation | 16 |
| Raw dispel (risky)                   | Arcana        | 18 |

- **Success path:** Free **2d4 modrons** (monodrones/quadrons); **no** Distortion penalty.
- **Failure on risky dispel:** **+1 Distortion**; **1d6** modrons **hostile** (confused) for 2 rounds until calmed (**DC 13 Persuasion**).

**Modron testimony (prime clue):**

> “Creator senses **sibling of chaos** wearing **order's mask** in the Stairway's weave. **Dead vigil** tower—**Everwatch**—pulls threads wrong.”

**Prep:** Temple map; ward rune handout; modron voices (clockwork, literal).

---

#### 3C — Griffon Eyrie (Halaster)

**Setup:** **Halaster Blackcloak** (or a **simulacrum** with 50% HP) stares at a **hairline crack in the sky** above the eyrie. He mutters about **Undermountain** “shifting when upstairs misbehaves.”

**Critical RP rule — inverted courtesy:**

| Party approach                             | Halaster reaction                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Kind, flattering, “wise sir”**   | He reads it as**false appeasement** → **attacks** (“You smell like **Chessex** and **lies**!”) |
| **Brusque, rude, honest, no titles** | He**relaxes** → shares intel                                                                                       |

**Intel (if friendly):**

- Stairway **misroutes** souls and **summons**; **Everwatch** “leaks.”
- Cyric's sigil **rhymes** with a spell he once saw in a **Zhent** tome (flavor).
- **Warning:** “Don't trust the **squirrel**—he gossips to **everyone**.” (Ratatoskr still helps; this is a red herring or half-truth.)

**Combat (if kind OR if they steal):**

- **Halaster** (archmage) or **clone** (half spells, no 9th level).
- **Goal:** Drive off, not kill—he **teleports** away laughing.

**Prep:** Cliff/eyrie map; Halaster one-liners; list of “insults” that count as respect.

---

### Act IV — The Stairway & Ratatoskr (20–30 min)

**Transition:** Regardless of entrance, the party steps onto the **Infinite Staircase**—stairs that **rise, fall, and branch** into star-mist.

**Encounter:** A **giant squirrel** blocks the path—then **bows**. **Ratatoskr** introduces himself (Odin's favor to Torm). **Climb on.**

**Ride mechanics:**

- **No navigation checks** to the **Nowhere Inn**.
- **Skill moment:** Each PC **one** **DC 12 Acrobatics** or hold on for **1d6** bludgeoning from a sharp turn (fail only).
- **Tyriel** refuses to ride at first (pride), then does—**awkward** comedy beat.

**Optional:** **Perception DC 15** during ride: glimpse **Everwatch** far above/below like a **broken lighthouse**.

**Prep:** Abstract stair map or theater-of-mind; squirrel mini or token.

---

### Act V — The Nowhere Inn (60–90 min)

**Description:** A **sprawling camp** of tents, stalls, and impossible architecture—**lodging between nowhere and somewhere**. **Overcrowded** with **refugees** misrouted from the distortion.

**Goal:** **Investigation** to confirm source = **Everwatch** / Cyric.

#### 5.1 Investigation clock

Track **Clues** (need **3** to unlock Ratatoskr's willingness to run to Everwatch):

| Clue source                                     | Skill                  | DC | Clue gained                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Djinni innkeeper** “Mistress of Keys” | Persuasion             | 14 | “Dead god's tower**eats** destinations.”                          |
| **Githzerai monk**                        | Insight                | 15 | Saw**three-eyed** shadow (Cyric) near **upper landings**      |
| **Lost paladin of Helm**                  | Religion               | 13 | Everwatch**still wards** but **wrong**—like a **mask** |
| **Modron refugee** (if not from 3B)       | Arcana                 | 14 | “Chaos sibling in**order** skin”                                  |
| **Ratatoskr** (bribe nuts or insult him)  | Deception/Intimidation | 16 | Admits he knows path to Everwatch—**won't** until you have proof   |

**Each clue:** **−1 Distortion** (max once per clue type).

#### 5.2 Services (optional roleplay)

| Service           | Cost  | Effect                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Stable for nerves | 5 gp  | Remove 1 level**exhaustion** (one PC)                  |
| Planar scribe     | 20 gp | **Advantage** on next navigation check                 |
| False guide       | 10 gp | **Trap:** +2 Distortion unless **Insight DC 16** |

#### 5.3 Tyriel at the Inn

- Urges **caution** about Everwatch.
- If Gariel gets **close** to naming **Tyr** from dreams, Tyriel **deflects** (“Stories grow teeth on the Stairway”).
- **Insight DC 18** (guest or Gariel): Tyriel **fears** something above, not below.

**Prep:** List of 4–6 NPC tents; Inn map (chaotic fair); clue tracker on DM screen.

---

### Act VI — Everwatch & Cyric (60–90 min)

**Travel:** Ratatoskr **sprints** to **Everwatch**—the **Grand Watchtower** of fallen Helm. Sky is **grey gold**; stairs **orbit** the spire.

**Arrival:** Doors **open**—no lock Helm would have used. Inside: **empty armor**, **cold braziers**, **windows** that show **everywhere** (Cyric's far-seeing prize).

#### 6.1 Cyric's greeting

Cyric appears as **handsome stranger**, **three shadows**, or **Helm's face** (cruel). He thanks them for **clearing** the way.

**He sees Tyriel:** **True Seeing** equivalent—**disguise falls**. **Tyr** stands revealed (missing hand, **absolute** presence).

**Tyriel/Tyr reaction (choose or player-driven):**

| Reaction       | Play                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Flee** | Tyr**steps into light** and **vanishes** toward a **justice plane**—Cyric taunts Gariel: “Your keeper **ran**.”                         |
| **Stay** | Tyr**does not** fight Cyric directly; may **imbue** Gariel (§5.2 unlock) and **hold** the tower's **exit** so Cyric cannot escape easily. |

#### 6.2 Confrontation paths

**Parley (Cyric loves lies):**

- He offers Gariel **Everwatch** to rebuild Helm **under Cyric's sight**—**DC 16 Wis save** or **charmed** for 1 minute (no repeat if saved).
- **Truth:** He wants to **see all paths**; Morag is **useful idiot** (or **partner**—your canon).
- **Success condition:** Party **names** the corruption and **forces** Cyric to **release** one anchor (partial fix): Distortion **reset to 0** on Sword Coast entrances; **not** a full godslayer victory.

**Fight:**

- Cyric uses **illusions**, **counterspell**, **teleport** within Everwatch.
- **3 Lie lairs** (legendary): each destroyed/banished when party succeeds **DC 15 Investigation** to find **contradiction** in Cyric's boast OR deals **30+** damage to him in one round.
- At **0 HP** or **3 lairs down:** Cyric **dissolves** into laughter—“**I already won the view**.” Tower **partially** clears.

**Tyr imbues Gariel (if fight and Tyr stayed):**

- Narrative: golden light **from Tyr's palm** into Gariel's chest—**Helm's locked power** **stirs**.
- Mechanic: §5.2 unlock + Gariel **advantage** on saves vs. illusion for 24 hours.

#### 6.3 Ending outcomes

| Outcome                                   | World state                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cyric banished / bargain broken** | Stairway**partially** stable; Torm **pleased**; Tyr **debt** to Gariel      |
| **Cyric escapes with foothold**     | Everwatch**still tainted**; sequel hook                                                 |
| **Gariel accepts Cyric's offer**    | **Major** campaign fork—Cyric patron (only if player wants)                            |
| **Tyr fled**                        | Gariel knows**Tyr** hid something; Ashbone **Tyrin** reunion is **awkward** |

**Return:** Torm **recalls** them to Trueheart for **debrief** or they **walk out** nearest Sword Coast entrance (**1d4** days lost time optional).

## 9. Continuity Hooks

- **Morag:** Named in Torm's briefing; **modron** clue ties her ascent to Stairway.
- **Words of power:** A refugee sells a scroll “**word that binds a path**”—clue to main quest folder.
- **Tyrin at Ashbone:** Gariel returns knowing **Tyr** and **Cyric**; does he confront Tyrin before the party does?

## 10. DM Notes & Safety

- **Bane oath:** Session zero—confirm player **opt-in** for dark promise.
- **Halaster:** Comedy through **rudeness**—make sure the table understands the **inversion** so they don't feel punished for being polite OOC.
- **Tyr reveal:** Emotional; Gariel may feel **betrayed** by Tyriel—leave space for player reaction.
- **Cyric fight:** Deities at full power TPK tables—use **Lie lairs** and **retreat** logic.
