# 1. The Drowning Door

**Plane:** Elemental Plane of Water

**Tone:** Panic / survival horror

The party opens a door expecting a stone landing and instead spills directly into an endless dark ocean beneath violent waves.

There is no up.

Bioluminescent shapes drift in the distance.

### Mechanics

* PCs immediately begin holding breath.
* Visibility is poor.
* The exit door drifts like a glowing portal far away, slowly sinking.

### Complication

A massive silhouette begins circling them:

* dragon turtle
* ancient whale
* kraken eye
* something unseen

The creature never fully attacks. Fear comes from pressure and pursuit.

### Twist

The “surface” is an illusion. Swimming upward only takes them deeper. They must realize the exit lies  *below* .



# 2. Trenches of the Blood War

**Plane:** Avernus / Blood War Front

**Tone:** Metal as hell

The party emerges into a crater while devils and demons clash in every direction.

Infernal artillery rains from the sky.

Nobody notices them at first.

### Objective

Reach another Stairway door visible atop a shattered siege tower.

### Mechanics

Every round:

* random explosions
* charging demons
* infernal war machines
* collapsing terrain

The players are not important enough to fight directly — they are ants crossing a battlefield of gods.

### Fun Detail

A horned devil casually gives them directions mid-battle because “you mortals are VERY lost.”



# 3. The Meadow of Eternal Joy

**Plane:** Beastlands (corrupted by Cyric)

**Tone:** Creepy cute

Cute animals greet the party:

* rabbits
* deer
* foxes
* talking bears

Everything is soft, warm, and peaceful.

Too peaceful.

### Twist

Nobody is allowed to leave.

The animals gently insist the party stay forever:

> “There’s no pain here.”

The longer they stay:

* Wisdom saves vs magical complacency
* movement becomes difficult
* smiles become unnerving

### Escape

The exit only appears when someone intentionally harms the environment or breaks the illusion of comfort.


# 4. Maw of a Thousand Forms

**Plane:** Limbo / Far Realm bleed

**Tone:** Chaotic comedy horror

The party transforms immediately upon entering:

* gelatinous cube
* goblin
* flumph
* beholderkin
* animated armor
* giant hand
* ooze

### Objective

Escape a gigantic living maw chasing them through a morphing landscape.

### Mechanics

Each transformed body grants:

* one weird ability
* one weird limitation

Examples:

* ooze can squeeze through cracks
* flumph floats
* mimic sticks to walls
* goblin becomes hyper-fast

### Twist

The maw speaks in the PCs’ own voices.


# 7. Carnival of Forgotten Gods

**Plane:** Wandering divine remnant

**Tone:** Beautiful melancholy

A carnival operated by dead minor deities no one worships anymore.

Games include:

* catching falling stars
* guessing forgotten prayers
* trading memories

### Mechanics

The gods desperately want attention.

Every interaction costs tiny memories:

* favorite food
* childhood pet
* first kiss
* smell of rain

### Reward

One god recognizes Helm’s essence within Gariel and offers cryptic warning:

> “The Watchtower still remembers duty.”



## 1. Aulivor, God of Last Chances

**Appearance:** Elderly man in patched royal robes carrying broken crowns

**Booth:** *The Wheel of Almost*

A massive wheel spins endlessly.

Every segment says things like:

* ALMOST
* NOT YET
* CLOSE
* AGAIN
* SECOND TRY

### Game

Each player rolls:

* 1d20
* then may choose to reroll repeatedly

But every reroll costs:

* 1 memory
* OR 1 hit die
* OR 5 max HP until long rest

### Goal

Hit exactly  **17** .

### Rewards

* Gain one free reroll later in the adventure
* OR reduce Distortion by 1

### Flavor

Aulivor whispers:

> “Mortals live on second chances. Gods die without them.”

# 2. Nemeia, Goddess of Childhood Monsters

**Appearance:** Gentle giant woman with stitched doll-face mask

**Booth:** *Under the Bed*

Children’s laughter echoes from beneath a tiny carnival tent.

Inside is magical darkness.

### Game

Each player enters alone.

Inside they confront:

* a childhood fear
* recurring nightmare
* symbolic insecurity

### Mechanics

Wisdom save DC 15 OR roleplay confronting fear.

Creative roleplay grants automatic success.

### Rewards

Success:

* advantage on next save vs fear or illusion

Failure:

* frightened for 1 minute after exiting

### Twist

Nemeia feeds on fear remembered fondly:

> “Children need monsters. Otherwise they never learn bravery.”

# 3. Veltrassa, Goddess of Lost Songs

**Appearance:** Blind celestial bard with silver tears

**Booth:** *The Hall of Echoes*

Ghostly music fills a mirrored tent.

### Game

Players hear fragments of songs from their lives.

They must continue the song:

* singing
* humming
* describing memory attached to it

### Mechanics

Performance or History DC 14.

If a player actually sings at the table:

automatic success.

### Rewards

* inspiration
* or temporary blessing:
* once reroll failed charm/illusion save

### Failure

The song is forgotten forever.

Describe the PC feeling emptiness where the memory belonged.

# 4. Dornak, God of Honest Duels

**Appearance:** Massive armored warrior missing his faceplate

**Booth:** *The Duel of Truth*

A dueling platform surrounded by spectral cheering crowds.

### Game

Players duel phantom warriors.

But:

* AC is ignored
* damage is ignored

Instead:

Each round players must answer a personal question honestly.

Examples:

* “Who do you envy?”
* “What would make you betray your oath?”
* “Who do you fear disappointing?”

### Mechanics

Deception automatically fails.

Each honest answer deals “truth damage” to opponent.

### Reward

Gain:

* immunity to magical lies for 1 hour
* advantage vs Cyric illusion later

### Flavor

Dornak says:

> “Steel never won battles. Truth did.”

# 5. Ishara, Goddess of Small Kindnesses

**Appearance:** Tiny old woman serving tea

**Booth:** *The Teahouse Between Worlds*

No game.

Just tea.

Most players will ignore this booth.

That is intentional.

### Mechanics

If players sit and genuinely talk with her:

* remove exhaustion
* heal emotional wounds
* reduce Distortion by 2

If they rush:

nothing happens.

### Secret

She is the most powerful entity in the carnival.

She survived because mortals still perform small kindnesses every day.

# 6. Tharos, God of Glorious Failure

**Appearance:** Burned knight carrying snapped banner

**Booth:** *The Leap*

A floating platform over endless void.

### Game

Players must leap impossible gaps.

### Mechanics

The jump is mathematically impossible.

The trick:

you only succeed if you leap *knowing* you may fail.

If players over-plan:

they fall safely back.

If they commit:

magic carries them.

### Reward

Gain:

* advantage on one reckless/desperate action later
* OR inspiration

### Flavor

> “Triumph is remembered. Failure shapes souls.”

# 7. The Empty Booth

No god.

No attendant.

Just a sign:

> RESERVED FOR THE GOD WHO RETURNS

Inside:

* old Helm symbols
* extinguished lantern
* untouched throne

Gariel may feel Helm’s resonance strongly here.

If he places:

* weapon
* prayer
* gauntlet symbol
* or speaks honestly

The lantern briefly reignites.

Everyone hears distant thunder.

Reduce Distortion by 1.
